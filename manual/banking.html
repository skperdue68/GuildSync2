Manual / Banking

Banking and tickets

Guild bank deposits are the record of everything: they buy raffle tickets, produce receipts, and feed the trading records.

Bank Deposits / Raffle Tickets

The tab lists guild bank deposits collected by GuildSyncBanking: who deposited, when, how much, and what the deposit was for. Search and filters narrow the list; totals update with the filters.

Deposit amounts ending in the raffle markers buy raffle tickets. Everything else is recorded as an ordinary deposit and never buys a ticket.

Bonus tickets do not add prize gold. Early purchase bonuses are optional and set per raffle type or per raffle in Raffle Bonus Tickets.

Receipt mail

GuildSync writes raffle receipts into ESO mail through the add-on. When a batch is ready, the tab shows a Write receipt mail button with how many mails are waiting and when the batch is available.

  1. Close ESO completely. Mail cannot be written while the game is running.
  2. Click Write receipt mail in GuildSync and let it finish.
  3. Reopen ESO. The add-on sends the queued mail.

A receipt shows the tickets bought and, when the purchase earned a bonus, the bonus percentage, the buy-before deadline and the total. If an earlier period would have paid more, the receipt ends with a nudge naming the better percentage and how many extra tickets an earlier purchase would have earned.

Duplicates and corrections

Add-on event IDs prevent the same deposit being recorded twice, even if a file is uploaded again or a collection restarts. If a deposit genuinely appears twice, treat it as a data question: check the deposit's event ID and time before changing anything, because removing the wrong row changes someone's ticket count.

What happens to the raffle spreadsheets

GuildSync fills the working raffle spreadsheet as purchases arrive, but only when the purchase belongs to the raffle that sheet is showing. Purchases for another period wait in the database until that period is loaded; they are never lost.

Archiving copies the workbook into the archive folder, saves the supported result fields, then resets and reloads both working tabs. Borders stay; the managed fills and formatting are cleared. Public announcements and archive notifications are separate optional features.

Purchase announcements

With purchase announcements enabled, the bot posts a message when a deposit buys tickets. Amounts and ticket counts come from the recorded deposit, so the announcement and the receipt agree.

Exports

Filtered banking data can be exported for a spreadsheet or a message to another officer. The export follows the filters currently applied, so narrow the list first if you only want one raffle or one member.
