Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions that come up most often. Each one links to the section that explains the whole process.

How do I handle a manual raffle ticket sale?

Record it as a banking entry of the raffle-ticket type, not as a plain deposit: the ticket maths only counts entries typed as a ticket purchase, and a plain deposit will never produce tickets.

  1. Open Bank Deposits / Raffle Tickets for the member.
  2. Add the entry with the amount the member paid and the date it was taken.
  3. Set the type to the raffle ticket purchase, so the entry carries the current bonus rules.
  4. Confirm the ticket count in the same row before moving on: it should match what you told the member.

If the sale was cash in hand and never went through the bank, say so in the entry's notes. The next officer reading the receipt list needs to know why there is no matching bank line.

See Banking and Raffles — ticket maths.

How do I move a banking entry from one type to another?

Edit the entry's type on the deposit itself; do not add a second entry to cancel the first. Changing the type recomputes the tickets for that entry, and the member's total follows.

  • Find the entry in Bank Deposits / Raffle Tickets and open it for editing.
  • Change the type (for example from a plain deposit to a raffle ticket purchase), then save.
  • Check the member's ticket total afterwards. A wrong type is the most common reason a total looks off by a whole entry.
  • If the entry came from an uploaded file, the next upload of that file can bring the old type back. Correct the source before re-uploading.

See Banking.

A member shows as a possible link but never links itself

Open the report's ? action on that row. The diagnostics panel scores every Discord member against the ESO account and says why the pair was or was not taken:

  • Would auto-link — the matcher should have linked it; run auto-linking again.
  • Would offer as a candidate — it scored above the 82% floor and waits for a person to accept it.
  • Held back by an existing link or block — another pair owns one of the two sides; resolve that first.
  • Opted out of matching — the member told us not to match them; leave it alone unless they ask.
  • Below the floor — the names are not close enough; accept it by hand if you are sure.

See Members — linking ESO accounts to Discord.

Why can't I remove a link?

GuildSync only offers unlinking when the link could be rebuilt afterwards. If the member said they no longer play ESO or do not use Discord, or they are no longer on the roster, the member link dialog explains that unlink is disabled instead of showing the button. Removing that link would leave nothing to restore it from.

If the link is genuinely wrong, correct the member's roster record first, or ask an administrator. See Members — linking.

A member told us they don't play ESO or don't use Discord

Record it on their member record rather than deleting anything. The opt-out keeps their history, stops the matcher offering them as a match, and keeps their existing link visible while blocking removal of it.

See Members.

Who changed this link, and when?

Use the clock action on the row in the links report. The history panel lists each change newest first with the states either side of it and the officer who made it; system changes show as System. Re-scoring a link without changing it is not recorded, so the list stays readable.

Which associates are ready for promotion?

Run Associate Members Eligible for Promotion. It lists two groups: Eligible for associates whose Discord link is in place, and Eligible if Linked for those who meet the tenure and ticket requirements but still need their Discord account reviewed. Fix the link in the links report first; the member then moves into the Eligible list.

See Reports.

Why won't receipt mail write?

ESO must be closed completely while GuildSync writes a mail batch. Close the game, click Write receipt mail, wait for it to finish, then reopen ESO. If it says no mail is available, nothing is queued yet.
