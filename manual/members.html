Manual / Members

Members and links

Two lists, one for Discord and one for ESO, joined by the link between a person's ESO account and their Discord account.

Discord Member Data

Everyone on your Discord server. Search by name, filter by role or link status, and sort any column. Each row shows the Discord name, server nickname, roles, whether the member is linked to an ESO account, and when they were last seen.

  • Click a column heading to sort; click again to reverse.
  • Click the history button on a row to open that member's Discord history with the name already filled in.
  • Refresh re-reads the list from Discord. Roles and members only change after a refresh, which the bot does with /roles.

Guild Roster

Everyone in the ESO guild, with rank, join date, notes, the linked Discord account and a history button. The roster updates when GuildSyncRoster uploads an ESO roster snapshot.

  • The Last Refresh time above the table tells you how current the data is.
  • The history button opens that member's roster history: rank changes, joins, leaves, and notes.
  • Notes are per member and visible according to your role. Use them for anything an officer needs to remember.

Member history

The history window lists every recorded change for one member, newest first, including rank and roster changes on the ESO side and joins, leaves and role changes on the Discord side. From a roster history, Users and Admins can also start an ESO account-name change.

You can open the window from the row button, or search for someone else once it is open.

Member links

A link connects one ESO account to one Discord account. GuildSync proposes links automatically by comparing names, and an officer confirms the uncertain ones. Raffle tickets, trader invoices and membership history all depend on the link being right.

StatusMeaning
linkedConfirmed. Either an exact automatic match or a pair an officer approved. A confirmed link is never overwritten by a later automatic pass.
candidateA suggestion the matcher is not certain about. It is listed in Possible links to make for review.
blockedThe pair is deliberately kept apart. It appears in Blocks we can remove.

The Member Links report

Open it from Reports. It groups links into three sections so each row has exactly one sensible action:

  • Possible links to make — candidates at 82% or better. The action is a check: approve and link the pair.
  • Blocks we can remove — pairs kept apart on purpose. The action is a restore arrow: let the matcher consider them again.
  • Links we can unlink — confirmed links. The action is a broken chain: remove the link.

Filters narrow by status, method and available action, and the search box matches either side of the pair.

Removing a link has two different outcomes, and the report says which one applies before you click.
  • An officer-approved link returns to Possible links to make as a candidate, marked → candidate.
  • An exact automatic link becomes a block, marked → block, which you can remove later from the same report.
You still get a confirmation dialog. Nothing is deleted from history.

Linking one member by hand

Open the member link dialog from a member row or from the report. It shows the ESO account, the current Discord match, the match method and confidence, and the actions available to you:

  • Approve a candidate (check) to confirm the pair.
  • Unlink a confirmed pair (broken chain) to remove the link.
  • Remove a block (restore arrow) to let the matcher consider the pair again.

Approved links are marked as confirmed by an officer, so the automatic matcher leaves them alone.

Member discovery and membership DMs

Member discovery helps officers find people who are on one side but not the other, so a member with no Discord account and a Discord member with no ESO account are easy to spot.

Where the membership DM campaign is enabled, GuildSync sends eligible unlinked Discord members a slow, alphabetical series of direct messages. Officers with the Consigliere or Kingpin role manage it with /membership-dm: start, pause, resume, restart, status, resolve and test.

Joining Discord and gaining access

When onboarding is enabled, a member with the Gangsters role is promoted to Associates once their Discord account is linked to an ESO account. GuildSync adds Associates, removes Gangsters and leaves your other roles alone. If you already hold a guild rank role, only Gangsters is removed and your rank is kept.

The confirmation arrives as a tagged message in a private thread in the onboarding channel. If private threads are unavailable, GuildSync uses a public thread in the same channel instead. No direct message is sent.

Members who stay unlinked after reminders are switched on receive one reminder after the configured delay, normally 24 hours. It is sent once per Discord member, including if they leave and rejoin.

ESO account-name changes

From a member's roster history, Users and Admins can choose Change ESO account name, preview the affected records and confirm. Viewers cannot rename. Previous names stay searchable, existing data stays attached, and the history records the old name, the new name, the operator and the time.

If the new name already exists in GuildSync, the rename is not merged automatically. Review the destination account first.
