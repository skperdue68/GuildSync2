GuildSync user manual

How to use GuildSync: reading the data, keeping ESO and Discord in step, buying raffle tickets, and finding your way around the desktop client.

What GuildSync does

GuildSync connects your guild's Elder Scrolls Online activity with Discord. ESO add-ons collect guild bank deposits and roster changes, the desktop client uploads them, and GuildSync stores the records. Those records produce raffle tickets, guild trader invoices, receipt mail, membership history and the reports officers review.

Data appears after an officer's ESO add-ons save it and the GuildSync desktop app uploads it. GuildSync never asks the live ESO bank: everything you see is based on what has been uploaded.

Your GuildSync role

GuildSync accounts are approved by an administrator. Roles decide what you can do here; they are separate from your Discord roles.

RoleWhat it allows
ViewerBrowse data, run reports, upload ESO history, and use automatic linking. New accounts start here.
UserEverything a Viewer can do, plus normal editing: notes, receipts, account-name changes and raffle work.
AdminEverything a User can do, plus administrator configuration, user management, and acting as another account to test permissions.
Some features also need a Discord role or an administrator switch. /gsr and /gsraffle in Discord require the Consigliere role; voice mute and onboarding each have their own switch in Administrator Configuration.

How to use this manual

  • Pages are grouped by the tab or task you are working in, not by which part of GuildSync does the work.
  • Screenshots are being added. Each one has a slot with a reference such as SHOT-MEMBERS-04; the caption says what the picture shows, so a page still reads correctly before the images land.
  • Commands are listed on the Commands page, split into Discord commands and in-game ESO commands. They are not the same thing: Discord /gsr manages raffles, ESO /gsr manages the roster.

If something looks wrong

Start with Troubleshooting. Most gaps come from one of three things: the add-on has not saved yet (type /reloadui in ESO), the desktop app has not uploaded yet, or the Discord account is not linked to the right ESO account.
