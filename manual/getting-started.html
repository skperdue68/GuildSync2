Manual / Getting started

Getting started

Sign in, get your role, and check that data is flowing. Everything else in GuildSync builds on those three steps.

Before you start

  • A GuildSync account. An administrator approves accounts. New accounts begin as Viewer.
  • Your ESO account name. GuildSync matches your ESO character account to your Discord account, so spelling matters.
  • Optional: the desktop client. The desktop app is what uploads ESO add-on files automatically. The web client shows server data and accepts uploaded files, but it cannot watch your ESO folder.

Signing in

  1. Open the GuildSync client and choose Sign in with Discord.
  2. Your browser opens Discord's authorisation page. Approve it and return to GuildSync.
  3. If your account is still waiting for approval, GuildSync says so. Ask an administrator to approve you; you do not need to sign in again after they do.
The desktop client keeps its sign-in. Closing the window to the tray or restarting the app does not sign you out. Use Log out in the profile menu when you want to end the session.

The first five checks

  1. Is your account linked? Open Discord Member Data and find your row. Linked should name your ESO account.
  2. Is anything uploading? In the desktop client, open your profile menu, then SavedVariables & File Watch. Each add-on file has its own switch and status.
  3. Is the roster current? Guild Roster shows the last refresh time above the table.
  4. Do your tickets appear? Banking lists deposits and the raffle tickets they bought. If they are missing, see missing tickets.
  5. Can you see the right tabs? Some tabs and reports are limited by role. The Reports page lists what each one needs.

The six tabs

TabWhat it is for
Discord Member DataEveryone on the Discord server: roles, nickname, linked ESO account, last seen, and history.
Guild RosterEveryone in the ESO guild: rank, join date, notes, linked Discord account, and history.
Guild ChatThe guild chat view. The web client shows it in the page; the desktop client opens it in your browser.
Bank Deposits / Raffle TicketsBank deposits, the tickets they bought, and receipt mail.
ReportsMember links, rank audit, last seen, associate tickets, community highlights and statistics.
AdminAdministrator Configuration, raffle bonus settings, and GuildSync user management.
Clicking a tab you are already on reloads its data. Background updates arrive on their own and keep your scroll position, filters and unsaved edits.

How data gets in

Three ESO add-ons write files that GuildSync reads: GuildSyncBanking (deposits), GuildSyncRoster (members and ranks) and GuildSyncApplications (applications). A fourth, GuildSyncTrader, records trader sales.

  1. In ESO, the add-on collects events while you play.
  2. ESO writes the file when you type /reloadui, log out, or exit.
  3. The desktop client notices the new file and uploads it.

On the web client you can drop the same GuildSync*.lua files onto the page instead, and GuildSync uploads them the same way.
