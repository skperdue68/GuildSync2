Manual / Raffles

Raffles

Two raffles run side by side: the Bi-Weekly raffle and the 50/50. Your tickets come from bank deposits, and the draw is worked from a spreadsheet.

Buying tickets

Deposit gold into the guild bank with the raffle marker in the amount. The deposit markers explain the amounts: 501 buys one Bi-Weekly ticket, 2503 buys one 50/50 ticket, and so on.

Deposits are recorded when GuildSyncBanking uploads them, so a ticket bought seconds ago can take a moment to appear. Ask in Discord with /tickets to see your own purchases.

Early purchase bonuses

Bonuses reward buying early. Each raffle type, or each individual raffle, can have its own periods and percentages; an administrator sets them in Raffle Bonus Tickets.

  • Bonuses round down per purchase. Five tickets at 20% earns one extra, for six total.
  • Earlier periods can pay more, and the last period pays 0%.
  • Ticket entries added by hand do not earn an early purchase bonus.

Checking a raffle from Discord

Members can use /raffle for the current prizes, draw times and ticket counts, and /tickets for their own purchases. Both answer privately unless you add public:true. Ticket details stay private even when the summary is public.

Replies show two times for each period: the As of time, when GuildSync assembled the answer, and the Latest recorded transaction, the newest entry the automation has recorded. The transaction time can lag behind real deposits; it does not confirm that every deposit has been uploaded.

Running the raffle as an officer

Raffle administration needs the Discord role named exactly Consigliere. The commands are /gsraffle and its short alias /gsr — remember that in ESO /gsr is the roster add-on, a different thing entirely.

CommandWhat it does
/gsr loadClears and refills the working raffle sheets from the database, including saved result fields. Returns an Open Working Raffle button.
/gsr load date:092526Loads the raffle containing that date from its archive, leaving the working sheet alone. Creates an archive if none exists.
/gsr update date:092526Saves winners, attendance and other supported result fields from that archived sheet back into the database. It does not clear the sheet or import purchases.
/gsr archiveArchives the current sheet, saves its result fields, resets both working tabs and reloads current data. Returns archive and working-sheet buttons.
/gsr resetClears managed data and draw dates on both working tabs without deleting database records. Load afterwards to restore.

Dates use zero-padded MMDDYY: 100326 is 3 October 2026. 10/03/26 and 10-03-26 also work. If zeros are missing and the date is unambiguous, GuildSync shows the full date and asks you to confirm. Ambiguous values fail rather than guess.

If a raffle date is the start or end boundary of a Bi-Weekly period, you are asked to choose Starts on this date or Ends on this date. You have one minute; no answer cancels the operation and changes nothing.

Editing an old raffle

  1. Run /gsr load date:MMDDYY and answer the boundary question if it appears.
  2. Open the returned archive button and edit the supported winner, attendance, bonus-ticket or winnings-sent fields.
  3. Run the update command shown in the response, with the normalised date and the same boundary choice.

Archived files keep their file ID while you load and update them. Archive names use the Bi-Weekly ending date, such as 261010 Raffle. Archiving the live sheet again can replace an older archive with the same name and give the replacement a new link.

Times and time zones

Discord shows dates and times in your own time zone, while draw times are defined in Eastern time. Around a daylight-saving change, a draw can appear an hour earlier or later than you expect; the recorded draw time is the authoritative one.

Community highlights

/highlights shows the Community Highlights report: raffle regulars, guild trader leaders and Discord activity. Trader gold is never included in the ranking. Use public:true to post it visibly in the channel.

Announcements stay tidy

When the bot posts a raffle summary, or you run /raffle public:true, it removes its own older summaries in that channel and keeps the freshest one. Private ticket replies, archive notifications, other announcements and messages from people or other bots are left alone. The bot needs Read Message History in the channel.
